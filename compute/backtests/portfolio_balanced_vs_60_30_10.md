# Portfolio backtest — balanced profile vs a static 60/30/10 benchmark

Walk-forward, monthly rebalance, 2000-01-31 → 2024-08-31 (296 months). Point-in-time throughout: every allocation uses only information knowable at its rebalance date, and each month's return is the realised *forward* month.

## What this is, and is not

This measures the **portfolio layer** — the balanced tilt against the static 60/30/10 mix, and the degraded-fallback machinery — not the individual engines, which were validated in their own phases. The goal is *robustness evidence*: how the allocation behaves through stress, especially 2008 and 2020. It is **not** a performance claim, and nothing here is investment advice.

- **rates**: replayed for real via `backtest/replay.py` from the month-end Treasury
  curve fixture (fast, no fit — the Nelson-Siegel λ is frozen).
- **gold**: replayed for real, with the Markov regime chain refit point-in-time on an
  annual expanding window (mirroring `monthly_refit`, never seeing future data).
- **equity** and **money**: documented reduced-form PIT states — trailing-return
  momentum + trailing volatility for equity, the PIT short rate for cash — because
  the full FinEquity stack cannot be replayed 300× offline and its publication series
  does not reach back to 2000 in the committed fixtures. Both are computed strictly
  causally through the same PIT gateway, so the no-lookahead guarantee holds.

Both portfolios are priced off the **identical** asset return series (equity as S&P price + a flat 2%/yr dividend proxy, gold as the London PM fix, rates as a 10y-Treasury total-return proxy `carry − 8.5·Δyield`), so the relative comparison is unaffected by the proxy choices — which is the whole point.

## The stress windows (what this backtest is really for)

| Window | Portfolio | Total return | Max drawdown |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2008 GFC | balanced | -20.00% | -23.07% |
| 2008 GFC | 60/30/10 | -22.94% | -27.21% |
| 2020 COVID | balanced | +1.26% | -13.58% |
| 2020 COVID | 60/30/10 | +4.94% | -10.22% |

### Reading the windows

- **2008 GFC.** The balanced profile drew down -23.07% against the benchmark's -27.21% — a gentler decline of ~4pp. The mechanism is visible in the weights: as gold's hedge regime firmed and equity's trailing volatility spiked, the allocator leaned out of equity and into gold (Oct 2008: equity 47%, gold 22%, vs a 60/10 neutral).
- **2020 COVID.** Here the static benchmark slightly *outperformed* (+4.94% vs +1.26%, with a shallower drawdown): the crash and V-shaped recovery inside a single quarter is faster than a monthly rebalance can respond to, and a volatility-driven de-risk that fires after the drop then misses part of the snap-back. That is an honest limitation of monthly strategic allocation, reported rather than hidden — a backtest that only ever flattered the strategy would not be evidence.

## Degraded months

0 of 296 months needed a neutral fallback: rates, gold, equity and cash each produced a usable state on every rebalance date across the window (gold's chain has ample history from 1968, so it fits from 2000 onward). The degraded path — an engine going missing or stale, its asset pinned at neutral and the allocation flagged — is exercised directly by the chaos tests (`tests/portfolio/test_chaos.py` and `serving/test/p6-portfolio.spec.ts`) rather than by this window.
